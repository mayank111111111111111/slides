Fixed Moment 2 · The Account Freeze

"Eduardo doesn't get the vision.
He's the problem."

  • Secret move to Palo Alto; his $19,000 spent without telling him
  • Response: freeze him out — no call, no meeting, no feedback
  • Result: trust collapses on both sides
  • Fixed leaders change the person. Growth leaders change the approach.

Speaker 6 · 0:45
Impact · Two Timelines

What actually happened

  • Co-founder erased, sued for years
  • Trust destroyed at every level
  • Mark wins the money, loses the friend
  • "I was your only friend."

With a growth-minded leader

  • Co-founder developed, retained
  • Culture of feedback from day one
  • Mark wins the money and keeps his friend
  • "We built this together."

The product succeeded in both timelines. Growth leadership changes the cost of success.

Speaker 7 · 0:45
Lessons for Future Engineers
01if (teammate.fails) → debug the process, not the person
02never let a label replace a real conversation
03growth(product) !== growth(people) // Mark had one, not the other
04ask: "What can I change in MY approach?"

Mark wasn't a bad engineer. He just refused to debug himself.
